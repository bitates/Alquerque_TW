Rules

  • Turns: Players alternate turns, making either a move or a capture.
  • Movement: A player can move a piece to an adjacent position if:
    • There is a line connecting the starting and ending positions;
    • The move does not step backward toward their own starting row.
  • Single and Multiple Captures:
    • An opponent's piece is captured by jumping over it into an empty position connected by a line (jumping backward is allowed);
    • Pieces in the back row can capture sideways or backward;
    • The captured piece is removed from the board;
    • Whenever possible, capture is mandatory.
    • Multiple captures can be made in a single turn:
      • Jump directions can change with each capture;
      • The piece cannot visit the same position twice;
      • The piece cannot capture the same opponent's piece twice;
      • Once a capture sequence begins, it is mandatory to continue capturing as long as possible.
    • Path Choice: If multiple alternative capture paths exist, any path may be chosen (it does not have to be the one that captures the most pieces).
  • Huff (Penalty): Occurs at the beginning of the turn, before moving or capturing, if:
    • The player failed to make a mandatory capture and moved a piece instead;
    • The player made a capture, but a multiple capture was possible.
    • Penalty: The opponent can "huff" (permanently remove) 1 piece belonging to the offender;
    • Even if multiple pieces could have captured, only 1 piece is huffed.
  • End of the Game: The game ends and the player loses if:
    • They have no pieces left to play;
    • They have no valid moves left (neither movements nor captures).
  • Draw: The game can end in a draw by mutual agreement, for example, when:
    • Captures can no longer occur (pieces cannot move backward and a blocking row exists);
    • No captures have occurred for many turns.
